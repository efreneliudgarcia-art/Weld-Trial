How this works: for each level, the trainer works out heat input from average current and travel speed (arc volts come from your arc gap). It compares that with the heat your UA worksheet's Level 1 puts in for this tube size and wall, adds heat build-up as the tube warms up, and adds gravity at the top and bottom. It's a teaching model, not a physics simulation. Machines run hot or cold, so a program that welds well on your machine can still read a little off from the UA baseline. It doesn't know your fit-up, arc gap, tungsten, purge pressure or material heat, all of which change real welds. A picture can't confirm internal soundness. Accept or reject always comes from your WPS and your inspector.

Purge cheat sheet backpurge before striking the arc

Recommended time = the longer of two common rules: the NCPWB field formula (4 × pipe diameter × feet of pipe ÷ CFH = minutes) and 5 air changes of the purged volume, with a 30 s minimum. Flow is estimated from the target Magnehelic reading and the exhaust hole size. Your current tube size is highlighted.

Exhaust holes: there's no single public industry table. Arc Machines' orbital training says to measure and record the exit hole for each tube size and tune flow until the gauge gives a flat, fully penetrated root. 1/4" and 1/2" (3/32" and 1/8") are back-calculated from your 14F and F 12 receipts (10.6 CFH at 3.5" and 1.5"). 1/4" on 1" to 4" and 3/8" on 6" are your shop's sizes. The 3/8" and 3/4" tube holes are starting guesses, so measure and record your own. Without an oxygen meter these times are minimums. High-purity specs usually require measuring oxygen, so follow your WPS.

How to use this trainer

The screen follows the SAXX-201 manual: Identification, Index, Programs list, the program screens, the START screen, Manual actions, Adjustments and Information. Your three work programs (14F, F 12, 2 INCH AB) are already in the Programs list.

Log in
Tap the user name to pick a user, tap the box next to the lock and type the password, then tap the lock. Factory passwords from the manual: ADMINISTRATOR 7890, WELDER 1234, OPERATOR 4567. An Operator can only read, print and weld.
Weld a program
Programs list › tap a program › Weld. That opens the START screen. Press START. The gear key switches between Weld, Test (rotation only, no gas, no arc) and Dynamic.
Gas and cooler
The rocker switches on the START screen and in Manual actions › Gas/Chiller really switch the solenoids. Torch gas shuts off by itself after 90 s. Internal gas keeps running and counts liters. Flow alarms are live any time gas is sent.
Make or change a program
Add walks you through Base, Mode, Electrode, Gas, Start, each Level and the Overlap, End and Save. In Add mode, changing the tube size recalculates everything from your UA worksheet rules. Options Prog. › Edit is Modify mode: a change touches only that value. The list key at the bottom jumps straight to any screen.
During the weld
STOP goes to downslope (press it again during downslope for a fast 2 s downslope). ALL STOP shuts down right away and raises alarm 0A. Any alarm stops the weld and switches to postgas, the same as the machine.
Energy
Same formula as the manual: E = 0.6 × U × I × 60 ÷ V, with U = 10 + 0.04 × I, using the pulse-averaged current and speed. For your 2 INCH AB level the trainer gives about 6,320 J/in. Your machine screen shows 6,305.
