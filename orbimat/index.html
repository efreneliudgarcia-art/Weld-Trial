How this works: for each level, the trainer works out heat input from average current and travel speed (arc volts come from your arc gap). It compares that with the heat your UA worksheet's Level 1 puts in for this tube size and wall, adds heat build-up as the tube warms up, and adds gravity at the top and bottom. It's a teaching model, not a physics simulation. Machines run hot or cold, so a program that welds well on your machine can still read a little off from the UA baseline. It doesn't know your fit-up, arc gap, tungsten, purge pressure or material heat, all of which change real welds. A picture can't confirm internal soundness. Accept or reject always comes from your WPS and your inspector.

Purge cheat sheet backpurge before striking the arc

  
Recommended time = the longer of two common rules: the NCPWB field formula (4 × pipe diameter × feet of pipe ÷ CFH = minutes) and 5 air changes of the purged volume, with a 30 s minimum. Flow is estimated from the target Magnehelic reading and the exhaust hole size. Your current tube size is highlighted.

  

  
Exhaust holes: there's no single public industry table. Arc Machines' orbital training says to measure and record the exit hole for each tube size and tune flow until the gauge gives a flat, fully penetrated root. 1/4" and 1/2" (3/32" and 1/8") are back-calculated from your 14F and F 12 receipts (10.6 CFH at 3.5" and 1.5"). 1/4" on 1" to 4" and 3/8" on 6" are your shop's sizes. The 3/8" and 3/4" tube holes are starting guesses, so measure and record your own. Without an oxygen meter these times are minimums. High-purity specs usually require measuring oxygen, so follow your WPS.

How to use this trainer

  
It copies the menu layout from the Smart Welder / Power Welder manual. A fresh copy opens with your 2" AB step program. The main menu has presets for every size from your UA worksheet (Regular, 4 levels is the sheet exactly), with Regular or Step and 1 to 4 levels. Your work programs 14F, F 12 and 2" AB, as they ran on your machine, are in Program Manager under Internal Memory / WORK, the same list your saved programs go to.

  

   
Adjust Program
Opens the parameter list: Basic Adjustments, Pre-Purge, Rotor Start Delay, each Level, Downslope, Post-Purge. Tap a value to change it. Values you changed but haven't saved turn yellow, the same as on the machine.

   
Apply value
Copies the selected sector value to every sector after it. Handy when every level uses the same pulse times or speeds.

   
Process graphic
The ring on the right. Each blue arc is a level (sector). Tap an arc to jump to that level's settings. The red marker is the start position.

   
Create New Program
Fill in tube size and wall, pick Your shop or UA worksheet, Regular or Step, and the number of levels, then press Calculate. The real machine uses Orbitalum's own calculation, so its numbers will differ.

   
Weld Mode / Test Mode
Press Start to watch the whole sequence play out: pre-purge, rotor start delay, each level with the current pulsing, downslope and post-purge. Test mode runs it with no arc, like the real Test mode.

   
Purge (ID)
Trainer-only screen for your backpurge: Magnehelic reading, exhaust hole, purged length and purge time. It estimates the flow, the air changes and the oxygen left, and shows how pressure and purge change the root in the Weld Result.

   
Weld Result
Below the screen. It updates as you change settings: the OD bead with ripples at your real pulse spacing, the root unrolled, cross-sections at 12, 3, 6 and 9 o'clock, and what each setting caused, with fixes.

   
Weld recipe
Under the Weld Result. Every setting in the current program, with the worksheet math (RPM, time for 360°, tie-in, UA rule checks) and tungsten length from the rotor OD you enter. It updates as you change settings, and changed values show in yellow. Copy sends it as text.

   
Program Manager
Your saved programs and your work programs, grouped by folder (WORK and PRACTICE). Tap one to load it. Save puts the program back in its folder, shown under the list. Everything is kept in this phone's browser only.
